How Offline Sync Works

1

Works 100% Without Internet

When you are in remote field areas without signal, simply keep entering data. Rows are stored in your phone's safe offline storage.

2

Satellite GPS Always Works

Phone GPS uses direct satellite radio signals, not mobile data. You can capture exact coordinates anywhere on Earth.

3

Automatic Sync on Reconnecting

As soon as your phone gets 3G/4G or Wi-Fi, GeoSync automatically uploads all pending rows to your Google Sheet without you lifting a finger!
